LinkedIn Import

How to export your LinkedIn connections:
1. Go to LinkedIn → Me → Settings & Privacy
2. Click Data Privacy → Get a copy of your data
3. Select "Connections" only and click Request archive
4. You'll get an email in ~10 minutes with a download link
5. Download and unzip — find the file called Connections.csv
6. Drop it below
